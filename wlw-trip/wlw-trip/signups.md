🌸 WLW Trip — Sign‑Ups & Attendance
Sapphic Society | Luxx Collective
This page tracks interest, sign‑ups, and confirmed attendees for the WLW Trip. It helps us plan lodging, transportation, activities, and safety accommodations. Updates to this page will automatically announce through the Luxx Collective Discord webhook.

✍️ How to Sign Up
To join the WLW Trip, members must complete the following steps:

Fill out the WLW Trip Interest Form  
(Form link will be added once created.)

Confirm availability for the trip dates  
Dates will be posted in trip-info.md.

Provide emergency contact information  
Required for all attendees.

Review the WLW Trip Rules & Safety Guidelines  
See rules.md.

Join the WLW Trip Discord channel  
For updates, announcements, and coordination.
