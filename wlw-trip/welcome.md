Welcome to the official WLW Trip hub for the Sapphic Society — a space built for connection, joy, safety, and community among women who love women. This page introduces the purpose of the trip, outlines our goals, and sets the tone for planning and participation.

🌿 Purpose of the Trip
This trip exists to create a dedicated, affirming space for WLW to connect, rest, explore, and build community. It’s designed to be inclusive of all women in the sapphic spectrum — lesbian, bi, pan, queer, questioning, trans women, and nonbinary femmes who align with WLW spaces.

Our purpose is simple:

Celebrate WLW identity

Build friendships and community

Create safe, joyful experiences

Offer space for healing, bonding, and fun

Strengthen the Sapphic Society within Luxx Collective

🎯 Trip Objectives
This trip is organized around clear goals to keep everything intentional and community‑centered:

Connection: Create opportunities for WLW to meet, talk, and bond.

Safety: Maintain a protected, respectful environment for all attendees.

Joy: Center fun, creativity, and shared experiences.

Empowerment: Encourage confidence, identity pride, and community visibility.

Organization: Provide clear schedules, updates, and resources through this hub.
